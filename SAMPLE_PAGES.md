## Dock Posted — How it works

# Three short walks.

The number on the pump. If they haven’t written one, we leave it blank — that just means pick up the phone.

### Your dock · $299 a season
Boats look here before they leave the ramp. You write the number when the truck comes. We never invent one.

1. Tell us which marina you run. That card becomes yours.
2. You write the price. Truck day, or when you change the board. Not every morning.
3. Someone leaving the ramp sees what you wrote. If you haven’t, they see a blank and a phone.

Button: **This is my dock**

### This trip · $29 a year to watch
Before you leave, see what a tank will cost on the water you fish. Charter or trailer. We only multiply a number they wrote.

1. Pick the water.
2. Enter the tank — gallons, or GPH and hours.
3. See what they wrote. No number means no number.
4. Leave an email if you want a note when those docks put a price up. Not a text.

Button: **Price this trip**

### Yard seats
When they name a storm, yards fill up. Who still has a seat. You call the yard. We don’t pull her.

1. File the boat — length, beam, home dock.
2. Two yards that fit. We do not invent a hole.
3. When they name it, we tell you what’s left, plus the yard phone.
4. You call them.

Button: **File the boat**

Texas through the Seychelles. Same 1–10. Not a bite. Pick a water. This page is the whole morning.

### Galveston
Redfish today. Fish the wind-blown shoreline — here the wind is the tide. Too hot in the middle. First light, or last light.

### Venice
Redfish today. Flood the west-bank ponds, then the south passes if the bulls are in.

### Islamorada
Permit today, if the light is right. Oceanside, or Channel 5 current. Not a Texas marsh sentence. First light, or last light.
